# General IT Acceptable Use and Support Policy

Corporate technology resources are provided for business purposes and must be used responsibly, lawfully, and in alignment with company values. Users are expected to protect company data, follow security guidance, and report issues quickly to reduce operational impact.

Users must keep systems updated and restart devices when security patches require it. Disabling endpoint security tools, tampering with monitoring controls, or attempting to evade policy enforcement is prohibited.

Support requests should be submitted through the IT ticketing system with clear symptoms, business impact, and relevant screenshots or error messages. Incident severity is assigned by IT based on business impact, scope, and urgency. Critical incidents may trigger escalation workflows and communications updates.

Confidential information must only be stored and transmitted using approved systems. External sharing of internal documents requires authorization and appropriate data handling controls. Use of personal email or unapproved cloud storage for company data is not permitted.

Remote work users must secure home networks with current firmware and strong Wi-Fi passwords. Public Wi-Fi should be avoided for sensitive tasks unless corporate VPN is active.

Repeated policy violations may result in access restrictions, disciplinary action, or legal escalation. Questions about acceptable use should be directed to IT Governance or Security before proceeding.
