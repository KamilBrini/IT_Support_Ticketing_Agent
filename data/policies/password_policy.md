# Password and Account Security Policy

All user accounts must follow the enterprise password standard. Passwords must be at least 14 characters, include a mix of character types, and not contain easily guessed words such as company names, user IDs, or seasonal phrases. Passphrases are encouraged when they meet complexity and uniqueness requirements.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all business-critical systems. Users must not approve unexpected MFA prompts. Suspected MFA fatigue attempts must be reported immediately through the security incident channel.

Passwords may not be reused across corporate and personal services. Storing passwords in browsers without enterprise-managed protection is prohibited. The approved enterprise password manager should be used for credential storage and rotation reminders.

Service accounts require unique credentials, least-privilege access, and owner assignment. Interactive login is disabled for service accounts unless specifically approved. Rotations for privileged and service credentials must follow the published rotation schedule.

Account lockout is triggered after repeated failed login attempts. Unlock requests require identity verification through approved methods. Requests involving another person’s account, unclear ownership, or signs of social engineering are escalated to the security operations team.

Any credential exposure, phishing submission, or suspicious login event must be reported within one hour. IT may force password resets and session revocation to contain risk.
