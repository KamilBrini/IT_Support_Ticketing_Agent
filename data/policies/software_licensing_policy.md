# Software Licensing and Application Use Policy

All software installed on corporate devices must be licensed, approved, and sourced through authorized channels. Employees must use the software request process for new applications, version upgrades, and renewals. Direct downloads from unknown sites are prohibited.

License assignments are role-based and should reflect business need. Managers and application owners review assignments quarterly to remove inactive or unnecessary seats. Shared named-user licenses are not allowed unless explicitly supported by vendor terms.

Open-source components used in internal tooling must comply with enterprise legal and security review requirements. Teams must document component name, version, license type, and known vulnerabilities before production use.

Applications that process regulated or sensitive data require data classification review and, when applicable, vendor security assessment. Unsupported, end-of-life, or unpatched software may be blocked by endpoint controls.

Users must not bypass software controls by installing portable executables, unauthorized browser extensions, or script-based loaders. Exceptions require risk acceptance from Security and Engineering leadership and must include a sunset date.

At employment termination or role change, software access is adjusted promptly. IT and procurement maintain records of entitlements, renewals, and audit evidence to satisfy vendor and compliance obligations.
