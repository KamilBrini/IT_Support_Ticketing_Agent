# Hardware Request and Replacement Policy

Employees may request standard hardware through the IT service catalog. Standard offerings include laptop models, docking stations, monitors, keyboards, mice, and approved headsets. Requests outside the catalog require business justification and manager approval before fulfillment.

New-hire equipment requests should be submitted at least five business days before start date. Urgent requests are processed based on inventory and staffing constraints, with fulfillment priority for production-impacting incidents.

Hardware replacement is allowed for device failure, warranty events, or approved lifecycle refresh. Devices showing accidental damage may require additional review. Users must not attempt unauthorized repairs or component swaps that could affect warranty or security configuration.

All endpoint devices must be enrolled in enterprise device management before issuance. Security baseline controls include full-disk encryption, endpoint detection, patching policy, and remote-wipe capability. IT may delay delivery until enrollment and baseline checks pass.

Upon replacement, employees must return old hardware within ten business days unless local laws or legal hold requirements state otherwise. Returned assets are sanitized under approved media handling procedures. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately, and an incident ticket must include last known location and time.

Procurement exceptions and high-cost requests may require finance approval and architecture review.
